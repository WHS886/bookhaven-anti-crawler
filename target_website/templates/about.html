About BookHaven

Dedicated to providing the best online book shopping experience

Our Story

BookHaven was founded in 2018 by three computer science graduate students who shared a deep love for reading. We believe that every book is a door to a new world. From a small garage startup to an online bookstore with over 12,000 titles, BookHaven has always stayed true to its mission: making great books accessible to everyone.

In addition to physical books, we are actively expanding into e-books and audiobooks, working toward a complete digital reading ecosystem. As of 2026, BookHaven has served over 500,000 readers across more than 300 cities nationwide.
